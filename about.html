Our story

We turned decoration booking into a two-minute job

Somewhere in India right now, someone is calling their fourth decorator of the day. The first one didn't pick up. The second quoted a price and then changed it. The third sent photos that belonged to someone else's work — and the event is tomorrow.

Mehfil was built to end that scramble. We photograph every design from a real home setup, show you the final price upfront, and send a verified decorator who builds exactly what you booked — nothing improvised, nothing swapped out at the last minute.

No haggling. No follow-up calls. No last-minute disappointments. Just a room that looks like the photo, ready when you need it.
